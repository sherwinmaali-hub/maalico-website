Why on the ground

What you can verify in person is what you can rely on.

Desk research and video calls only go so far, especially in emerging and frontier markets. Facilities change hands, inventories move and partners overstate capacity. Maalico closes that gap by putting experienced people at the site, the port and the partner's office.

Presence

We deploy to the country and the site, meet the people involved face to face and see the yard, the warehouse and the paperwork ourselves.

The right team for each project

Every engagement is led by a senior Maalico principal and staffed with vetted specialists chosen for that market and scope, from logistics and HSE to legal and engineering partners.

Standards

Every engagement is documented the way a US board expects: signed reports, meeting minutes within 24 hours, photo and GPS evidence, opportunity and risk registers, and clear next steps.

Coordinating the import of equipment and materials from the United States and Western Asia through Port Harcourt, including customs interfaces, clearing agents, warehousing and inventory control.
